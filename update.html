Grading components

The scored parts of this subject (e.g. Midterm, Final). Each has a maximum raw score and a weight; a student's total is the sum of raw / max × weight. The sum of weights is the subject's total marks.

Changing a component's max or weight doesn't rewrite results that are already saved — re-save or re-import them to recompute totals.
